Why Warehouses Need Professional Waterproofing

Dubai warehouses store valuable inventory - electronics, textiles, chemicals, food products - often worth millions. A single roof leak can cause massive damage:

  • Inventory Loss: Water damage to stored goods creates total loss
  • Production Disruption: Factory shutdowns cost thousands per hour
  • Mold & Health: Moisture creates toxic mold affecting workers
  • Structural Damage: Water infiltration weakens concrete and steel
  • Insurance Issues: Preventable water damage may void insurance coverage

Our Warehouse Waterproofing Approach

For large industrial facilities, we use a strategic approach:

  • Phase-Based Work: Waterproof roof in sections so operations continue
  • Industrial Membranes: Heavy-duty EPDM or PVC systems for large areas
  • Drainage Rehabilitation: Fix gutters, downspouts and drainage patterns

Warehouse Waterproofing Questions

How long does warehouse waterproofing take?
Depends on roof size. A 5,000 sqm warehouse typically takes 2-3 weeks. We work in phases so operations continue. Large facilities may take 4-6 weeks if working only evenings/nights.
Will waterproofing affect our operations?
No. We coordinate work around your schedule. We can work nights, weekends or in sections. Most warehouses see zero operational disruption.
What's the cost for a large warehouse?
Industrial waterproofing costs vary by roof condition and size. Average range is AED 50,000 to AED 300,000+ for large facilities. We provide free detailed inspections and fixed quotes.
Can you handle HVAC systems and penetrations?
Yes. We properly waterproof all roof penetrations including HVAC units, skylights, vents, and exhaust pipes. This is critical for preventing leaks.
