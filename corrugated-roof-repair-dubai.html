Corrugated Roof Problems

Corrugated metal roofs are commonly used in warehouses, factories and industrial buildings across Dubai. However, they face unique challenges in UAE climate:

  • Fastener Corrosion: Screws and bolts rust and create water entry points
  • Metal Corrosion: Salt air and humidity cause rust streaks and deterioration
  • Seam Failures: Seams separate and allow water to seep through joints
  • Coating Breakdown: Factory coatings fail under extreme Dubai heat (50°C+)
  • Thermal Expansion: Temperature swings cause movement and stress on fasteners
  • Sealant Failure: Old caulk and silicone patches fail within months
