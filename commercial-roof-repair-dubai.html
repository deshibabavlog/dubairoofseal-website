The Permanent Fix

Why EPDM Rubber Membrane is the Right Solution

Most waterproofing failures on metal roofs happen because the wrong system was used. Silicone patches and bitumen tape fail within months because metal expands and contracts with Dubai's extreme temperature swings.

EPDM rubber membrane is different. It moves with the metal, creates a fully bonded watertight seal over the entire roof surface — screws, ridges, penetrations and all — and lasts 20+ years in UAE conditions.

🔗

Fully Bonded — No Gaps, No Entry Points

Adheres directly to metal surface. Covers every screw, ridge cap, gutter edge and penetration. No separate sealant strips that can fail.

♨️

Designed for Dubai's Extreme Heat

Withstands 120°C+ surface temperatures without cracking, bubbling or delaminating. Tested and proven in UAE climate conditions.

🏭

Works Around Your Operations

Applied in sections so your warehouse or factory can remain operational. We also offer night shifts and weekend application.

❄️

Reduces Internal Temperature

White or light-coloured EPDM reflects solar radiation, reducing internal warehouse temperature by 5–10°C and cutting AC costs.

How much does metal roof waterproofing cost in Dubai?
Metal roof waterproofing in Dubai starts from AED 15,000 for small warehouses and ranges up to AED 90,000+ for large industrial facilities. Cost depends on roof area, profile type, and existing condition. We provide a 100% free site inspection and a fixed written quotation — no surprises on the final invoice.
What is the best waterproofing system for a metal roof?
EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer) rubber membrane is the gold standard for metal roofs in Dubai. It bonds directly to the metal, covers all screws and joints, and has a proven 20+ year lifespan in extreme heat and UV. Silicone patches and bitumen tape fail quickly because they can't handle metal's thermal expansion — EPDM moves with the metal.
Can you waterproof while the warehouse is still operating?
Yes — this is standard practice for us. We work in sections so operations can continue. For factories with sensitive processes, we can schedule work during off-peak hours, nights or weekends. We coordinate with your operations team before work begins.
How long does metal roof waterproofing take?
Small warehouses up to 1,000 sqm typically take 3–5 days. Medium facilities of 1,000–5,000 sqm take 1–2 weeks. Large industrial roofs of 5,000+ sqm can take 2–4 weeks. We provide a clear written project timeline before work begins.
Will the waterproofing reduce heat inside my warehouse?
Yes. White or light-grey EPDM membrane reflects solar radiation instead of absorbing it, which can reduce the internal temperature of a warehouse by 5–10°C. This reduces cooling costs significantly — many clients report measurable savings on electricity bills after waterproofing.
My roof has been patched many times. Can you still fix it?
Absolutely. We regularly remediate roofs with multiple layers of failed patches. We strip and treat the surface properly, identify the real failure points, and install a complete EPDM system that covers everything — backed by our own 5-year warranty. Most clients who come to us have been patching for years with no lasting result.
