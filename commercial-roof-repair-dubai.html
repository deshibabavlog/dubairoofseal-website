Commercial Building Roof Challenges

Commercial properties face unique roof challenges that threaten tenant satisfaction and property value:

  • Tenant Complaints: Water leaks in offices, retail spaces, and hotel rooms damage reputation
  • Flat Roof Issues: Most commercial buildings have flat roofs prone to pooling water
  • HVAC Penetrations: Complex mechanical systems create leak points
  • UV Damage: Intense Dubai sun breaks down membranes and coatings
  • Property Value: Roof leaks reduce asset value and tenant appeal
  • Liability: Water damage creates legal liability for property owners
